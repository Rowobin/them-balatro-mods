**Erebos' Intervention**

**Heliod's Intervention**
- Gain money equal to the amount of money gained this ante. (Max 30)

- Draw all cards with the same rank as the selected card from the top 1 cards of your deck. Value increases by 1 for each card drawn after the first draw this round.
