### Instants

**Erebos' Intervention** ✓
- Reduce rank of card by 1 and gain 1$. Value increases by 1 for each card destroyed this game. (Max 13)

**Final Death**
- Destroy a card or joker (ignores eternal)

**Heliod's Intervention** ✓
- Gain money equal to the amount of money gained this ante. (Max 30)

**Karametra's Blessing**
- Selected card can't be disabled and is retriggered once when scored.

**Thassa's Intervention**
- Draw all cards with the same rank as the selected card from the top 3 cards of your deck. Value increases by 3 for each hand remaining.

**Thirst for Meaning**
- Draw three cards. Discard two unless you drew a 4 or a 2

**Final Flare**
- Select 2 cards. Destroy the left one and reduce the rank of the right one by the destroyed card's rank.

**Infuriate**
- First scored card gives +10 mult when scored

**Gift of Nature**
- Selected card gain +15 chips

**Inspiring Awe**
- Disable current boss blind (I never show up in shop again)
